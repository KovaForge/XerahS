## Summary

April 4 was a dense XerahS integration day that mixed architectural cleanup, dependency sync work, and Windows capture fixes. The day closed with two scrolling-capture fixes that redirected message-based scrolling to the actual content window and stopped child-window scrolling from switching tabs.
